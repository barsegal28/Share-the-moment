# Share the Moment — component library

When a kid earns 3 stars on a song, they can send the recording to family, and a parent can send a surprise back.
This Storybook holds every component of the prototype and each of its states.

**Source of truth:** the Figma file *Share the moment*, page **app asets**. Every component here is built from its
Figma node with the file's own values: sizes, padding, gaps, radii, text styles, tokens and effects.
**Language:** Sticker — die-cut vinyl stickers on an ink sheet. Every object gets a 9px cream border and a 12.5px
kiss-cut edge in a contrasting colour (3.5px visible), and a lift (7px down, 10px blur, 30% black). Instances scale
the edge and lift with the sticker, as Figma does. Riso grain sits on the sheet only.

## Sections

- **Foundations** — palette, type and effects (the Figma variables, text styles and effect styles).
- **Sticker** — `StickerArt`, the art library and the sheet.
- **Components** — the app components: Button, Play, Progress.bar, Reward progress, Player, Avatars, Stars,
  background, main (result card), share illustration and Parent.Mobile.

## Design rules for ages 6–9

- Voice-over on every prompt; text is a backup.
- People are faces, not names.
- One obvious action per screen; no typing.
- Every tap gets sound and motion.
- No negative signals for the kid.
- The kid always chooses to send; recordings are audio only.
